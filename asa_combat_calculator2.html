Planetary influence
The sky is read from the RP Calendar automatically. Empowered schools get +1 free cast before miscast risk; weakened (faint) schools get -1. Five schools follow their planet's orbit. Flames follows the season, empowered through summer and at either solstice; Memories follows Ashr, empowered at the Full Moon and faint at the New Moon.
